Donation Statement
Downloads a PDF statement — with your logo, site name, and a faint watermark — of donors whose last donation date falls within the selected range. Each row shows Name, Blood Group, Address, Hospital and Donate Date (no mobile numbers), followed by a summary with blood-group and hospital charts. Bangla text is rendered correctly.
